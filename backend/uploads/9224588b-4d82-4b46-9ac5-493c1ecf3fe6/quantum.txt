Quantum computing fundamentally exploits superposition and entanglement of qubits to achieve computational speedups over classical binary architectures. Unlike classical bits restricted to 0 or 1, qubits exist in coherent superpositions, enabling massively parallel quantum gate operations. Quantum circuits built from Hadamard gates, CNOT gates, and Toffoli gates manipulate qubit registers to implement algorithms like Grover's search and quantum phase estimation.
